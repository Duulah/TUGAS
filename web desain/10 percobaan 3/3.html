Percobaan 3: Embed YouTube Video

Video YouTube di bawah ini di-embed menggunakan iframe dengan responsive design.

Kode HTML:

<div class="video-container">
<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/dQw4w9WgXcQ"
frameborder="0"
allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope;
picture-in-picture"
allowfullscreen>
</iframe>
</div>
